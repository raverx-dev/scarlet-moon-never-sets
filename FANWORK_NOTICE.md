# Unofficial Touhou Project fan-work notice

**東方紅月夜 ~ Scarlet Moon Never Sets ~ is an unofficial Touhou Project fan game.**

Touhou Project, its characters, setting, concepts, and original Touhou musical compositions originate with **ZUN / Team Shanghai Alice**. This game is not an official Touhou title and does not imply approval, sponsorship, or endorsement by ZUN or Team Shanghai Alice.

**RAVER X DEV** is the developer/owner mark for this unofficial fan-game project, not the creator of Touhou Project, its characters, its setting, or ZUN's source musical compositions.

The graphics, sprites, backgrounds, portraits, bitmap lettering, animations, game code, sound synthesis, sound effects, original stage music, and arrangement implementation were newly created for this project. Approved reference artwork is used for visual direction, while RoboPixel carries canonical authored pixel state for Version 4 production art. No official game graphics, source code, or recordings are embedded or redistributed. No other fan creator's graphics, recordings, or arrangements are included. The executable does not download assets at runtime.

The Touhou compositions referenced by the musical plan are *Maiden's Capriccio*, *Beloved Tomboyish Girl*, *Love-Colored Master Spark* (including its shared *Love-coloured Magic* motif), and *Septette for a Dead Princess*. ZUN retains ownership of those source compositions. This release uses newly synthesized, abbreviated musical adaptations; the README records their verification limits.

No claim of ownership is made over Touhou Project, its characters or setting, or ZUN's source compositions.
