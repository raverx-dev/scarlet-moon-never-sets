# Touhou NES Fan Game — Story and Dialogue Script

# Story & Dialogue Script

## Title

**東方紅月夜**  
**~ Scarlet Moon Never Sets ~**

## Premise

For three consecutive nights, the moon has refused to set over Gensokyo.

Reimu initially investigates for one practical reason:

**Nobody is visiting Hakurei Shrine, and therefore nobody is donating.**

The actual cause is Remilia Scarlet.

Remilia arranged for a magical **Lunar Anchor** at the Scarlet Devil Mansion to hold the moon near midnight because she thought Gensokyo looked better that way.

Her motivation is almost completely aesthetic.

The disproportionate amount of violence used to resolve this disagreement should never be treated as unusual.

# STAGE 1 — MISTY LAKE

## CIRNO APPEARS

Cirno drops dramatically into Reimu's path.

**CIRNO:**  
STOP RIGHT THERE!

**REIMU:**  
Why?

**CIRNO:**  
Because I did it!

**REIMU:**  
Did what?

**CIRNO:**  
I FROZE THE MOON!

Pause.

**REIMU:**  
No you didn't.

**CIRNO:**  
I DID!

**REIMU:**  
You can't reach the moon.

**CIRNO:**  
I'M THE STRONGEST!

A tiny **⑨** briefly appears beside Cirno's portrait.

**REIMU:**  
You're in the way.

**CIRNO:**  
That's basically admitting I did it!

**REIMU:**  
Sure.

Pause.

**REIMU:**  
Hold still.

### BOSS — CIRNO

Fight.

---

## CIRNO DEFEATED

Cirno drops downward, then floats back up looking annoyed.

**CIRNO:**  
HEY! I wasn't finished being the strongest!

**REIMU:**  
Did you actually see anything strange?

**CIRNO:**  
No.

Pause.

**CIRNO:**  
Except that black-and-white witch.

**REIMU:**  
Marisa?

**CIRNO:**  
She flew toward the forest carrying some weird book.

**REIMU:**  
Useful.

**CIRNO:**  
So I helped solve the incident!

**REIMU:**  
Don't push it.

Reimu exits.

Cirno:

**CIRNO:**  
I'M STILL THE STRONGEST!

# ENDING

Hakurei Shrine.

Morning.

Bird-like pixel sprites cross sky.

Reimu sits beside donation box.

Nothing happens.

Long pause.

Remilia walks onto screen holding parasol.

She drops **one coin** into donation box.

Reimu looks at coin.

Looks at Remilia.

Looks at coin.

Long pause.

**REIMU:**  
...One?

**REMILIA:**  
Consider it compensation.

**REIMU:**  
For three days?

**REMILIA:**  
Don't spend it all at once.

Remilia walks away.

Reimu stares directly toward screen.

Freeze frame.

Short ending sting.

**THE END**
