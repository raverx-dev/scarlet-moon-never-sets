**The workflow is proven at this scale. The visual result supports the intended direction, but is not a final Mansion art pass.**

| Criterion | Assessment | Evidence / limitation |

## What still falls short

- **Depth is approximate.** The runner widens and side architecture changes position, but the near/far windows retain one native size. The diamond floor does not converge to one vanishing point. A larger pass needs separately authored depth variants rather than scaling these pixels.
- **The rose is still a small emblem.** It establishes location identity, but could carry stronger asymmetric glass detail and a richer surrounding end-wall treatment.
- **Symmetry/repetition remain visible.** This is a tight reusable-kit proof, not the complete range of near/far bays, vaults, drapery and deliberate exceptions required for a finished room. The revised masonry reduces repetitive panelling but does not eliminate all regularity.
- **Gameplay readability is static evidence only.** The centre is low contrast and untouched V3 sprites remain legible in the scale witness. Dense live bullets, motion, boss overlays and the full scrolling behavior have not been tested or integrated.
- **Story staging needs runtime review later.** Two actors fit above the existing y=174 dialogue boundary; the current proof uses a coverage rectangle, not actual dialogue interaction or animation.
- **Pixel lint has warnings.** Retained unwaived in the evidence. Several are intentional diagonal/stipple details, but a production pass should assess them visually at native size rather than blanket-waiving them.

## Recommendation

Recommend a **larger but bounded Mansion-only refinement pass after Owner visual review**, focusing on depth variants, floor convergence, and stronger apse identity. Keep this canonical-asset/exact-export workflow. Do not infer authorization for that next pass from this recommendation, and do not treat this proof as permission for full V4 production or game integration.
